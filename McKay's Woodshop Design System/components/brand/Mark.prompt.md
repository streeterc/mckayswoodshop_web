One-sentence: a single glyph from the logo grid, used as a section stamp or divider ornament — at most one per screen region.

```jsx
<Mark glyph="buck" tone="copper" size={32} assetBase="../../assets" />
```

Glyphs: `cross`, `pine`, `buck`, `lumber`. Tone `copper` only on forest-green fields. Do not use these as functional icons or button glyphs.

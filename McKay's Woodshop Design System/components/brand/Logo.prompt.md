One-sentence: the brand lockup — use it in headers, footers, print marks and slide title cards; never typeset the brand name as a substitute when artwork is available.

```jsx
<Logo variant="knockout" height={48} assetBase="../../assets" />
```

Variants: `full` (copper on the forest-green field — the default, and the only one to use at small sizes), `knockout` (copper shapes on transparent, for placing on forest green or photography), `mark` (the four-tile grid on its copper field — favicon, stamp, loading state), `mark-green` (the four glyphs in forest green on transparent, for light surfaces and single-colour print). Minimum size for `full` is 40px tall; below that use `mark`.

A quote request costs nothing and comes with no obligation — it's a conversation, not a contract. The first message just needs a rough idea of what you want, roughly how big, and roughly when you need it by. From there it's usually a short back-and-forth (a call, some photos of the space, a sketch) to firm up wood species, dimensions, and finish before a price gets attached to anything.

As a rough guide, dining tables typically start around $2,500 depending on size and wood; built-ins and full kitchens vary more, since they depend on the space itself. Every quote is specific to the piece — nobody's publishing a price list for custom furniture, because no two commissions are really the same job.

Lead time is the other thing people ask first. A single piece — a table, a bench, a set of shelves — usually runs a few weeks in the shop once the design is settled; built-ins and multi-piece kitchens take longer. Delivery is local around Simcoe County and Barrie; pieces that need to travel farther can ship. None of that gets firmed up until the quote stage, but it's rarely the surprise people expect it to be.

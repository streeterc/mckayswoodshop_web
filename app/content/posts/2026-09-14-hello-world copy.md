A client wanted a console long enough to run the length of their entry wall — 96 × 18 × 30 in, in rift-sawn white oak with no visible fasteners. Rift-sawn was the right call for the grain: straight lines the whole length of the top, no cathedral figure to fight when the boards were laid side by side.

The case is joined with through-tenons at the legs and a full-length stretcher underneath. Everything above the stretcher is drawer — five of them, dovetailed, running on wood runners instead of hardware slides. Slower to fit, quieter to use.

Finish was hand-rubbed oil, four coats instead of the usual three. Oak this pale drinks the first coat unevenly, and it showed on the second. Third and fourth evened it out. Ready in nine weeks from first measure to delivery.

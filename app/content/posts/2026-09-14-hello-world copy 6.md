Starting next week, the shop opens at 7 instead of 8, Monday through Friday, and closes at 4. Saturday is still by appointment only. The change is about light more than anything — the bench sits under a bank of north-facing windows, and an extra hour of daylight in the morning matters more than one at the end of the day.

If you've got a standing appointment, nothing changes on your end; it's already been moved. Walk-ins are welcome during shop hours, though a call ahead means someone's actually at the bench and not out on a delivery.
